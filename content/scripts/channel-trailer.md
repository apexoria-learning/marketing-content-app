# Channel Trailer Script (~45 sec)

> Goal: tell a new visitor **who you are**, **what they'll get**, and **why trust you** — fast.
> Format: 🎥 ON CAMERA throughout (it's your intro / brand). Keep it warm, slow, confident.
> ⚙️ Follows [CONTENT-RULES.md](../CONTENT-RULES.md).

---

## On-screen text plan
- 0:00 — `Wishvesh Ujawane` + "Salesforce Technical Lead"
- 0:15 — bullet list of topics (Apex · LWC · Integration · Agentforce)
- 0:35 — "New video every [day] • Subscribe"

---

## EXACT SCRIPT — 🎥 ON CAMERA throughout

**[0:00-0:08] Hook + intro — say exactly:**
> "Salesforce seekhna chahte ho, bina theory ke jungle mein khoye? Toh aap sahi jagah aaye ho.
> Main hoon Wishvesh Ujawane, aur ye hai Trailhead Simplified."

**[0:08-0:22] Who you are / why trust — say exactly:**
> "Main ek Salesforce Technical Lead hoon — real projects pe kaam karta hoon, real problems solve karta hoon.
> Jo yahan sikhaata hoon, wo theory nahi — wo cheezein hain jo actually kaam aati hain."

**[0:22-0:38] What they'll get — say exactly:**
> "Is channel pe milega — Apex, LWC, Async Apex, integrations, aur Agentforce —
> sab kuch simple Hinglish mein, chhote aur seedhe videos mein. No fluff, no time waste."

**[0:38-0:45] CTA — say exactly:**
> "Har [day] ek naya video aata hai. Toh subscribe karo, aur chalo — Salesforce ko simple banate hain.
> Milte hain agle video mein!"

---

## Recording notes
- Deep breath before you start. Smile — it changes your voice.
- Pause after each sentence. The editor cuts the gaps; it won't cut the calm.
- Two takes max per line; pick the slower one.
- End frame: hold a smile for 2 sec for a clean outro cut.

## Publishing
- Set as **channel trailer** (Customize channel → Layout → For non-subscribers).
- Title: *"Welcome to Trailhead Simplified — Salesforce, simplified"*
- Pin a comment with your weekly schedule + a "what do you want to learn first?" question.
