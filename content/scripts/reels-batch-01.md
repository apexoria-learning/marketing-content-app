## Recording tips for reels
- Record vertical from the start (DemoCreator vertical canvas) — don't crop later.
- First 3 seconds decide everything: say the hook *while* the text appears.
- Keep one idea per reel. Speak a touch slower than feels natural.
- Always end with the same CTA style so your brand voice is consistent.
