## 6. Pacing / Speaking Routine (every recording)
1. Write **bullet talking points**, not a word-for-word script (sounds natural, prevents rushing).
2. Warm-up: read one paragraph aloud slowly + 3 deep breaths.
3. Record in short **1-2 min segments**; pause between thoughts.
4. End every sentence with a deliberate pause (editor trims the excess).
5. Playback at 1x and 1.25x — if it's hard to follow at 1x, re-record.
6. In DemoCreator: cut filler and **add captions** (helps fast speech comprehension).
- **Target speaking rate: ~130-150 words/min** (check: transcript word count ÷ minutes).
