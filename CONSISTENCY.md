# Consistency Engine

The single biggest reason channels die is **inconsistency**. This file is your system to beat that.

---

## 1. The Batch Model (your weekly rhythm)
Pick **one fixed "Studio Day"** each week. Do everything in batches instead of a little each day.

| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|----------|------|
| Studio Day (e.g. Sunday) | Script the week's video + reels (I draft, you refine) | ~45 min |
| Studio Day | Record video + reels back-to-back (warm voice, one setup) | ~60 min |
| Day after | Edit in DemoCreator + captions + thumbnail | ~90 min |
| Anytime | Schedule uploads (don't post manually) | ~15 min |

> **Total ≈ 3.5 hrs/week** — fits your 2-4 hr budget.

---

## 2. Buffer Rule (kills weekly panic)
Before you go public, build a buffer:
- **2 videos** ready in advance
- **4 reels** ready in advance

If a busy week hits, you publish from the buffer and your streak never breaks. Refill the buffer whenever you're ahead.
