# Content Production Rules

> **Every** video and reel for Trailhead Simplified MUST follow these rules.
> Creator: **Wishvesh Ujawane** — Salesforce Technical Lead.

---

## The 3 Lanes (every script is written this way)

Each script labels every moment with one of these lanes, so you always know what to do:

| Lane | Meaning | When |
|------|---------|------|
| 🎥 **ON CAMERA** | Face on camera | Intro hook, "who I am", recap, CTA/outro |
| 🎙️ **OFF CAMERA** | Voiceover only (no face) | The actual teaching |
| 🖥️ **SHOW ON SCREEN** | What to display while screen-sharing | During every teaching segment |

---

## Mandatory Video Skeleton
1. **🎥 ON CAMERA — Hook (0-15s):** pain-point question + signature line.
2. **🎥 ON CAMERA — Promise (15-40s):** the 3 things they'll learn.
3. **🎙️ OFF CAMERA + 🖥️ SCREEN — Teaching:** segments, each with talking points + exactly what to show.
4. **🎥 ON CAMERA — Recap + CTA (last ~30s):** 1-line recap + like/subscribe + a comment question.

## Mandatory Reel Skeleton (60s)
1. **Hook (0-3s):** bold claim/question — spoken + on-screen text (face optional).
2. **🎙️ + 🖥️ Body (3-50s):** one tip with a screen demo.
3. **CTA (50-60s):** "Follow Trailhead Simplified."

## Non-negotiable rules
- ✅ Hook in the **first 3 seconds**.
- ✅ Speaking rate **130-150 wpm**; pause after every sentence.
- ✅ **Burned-in captions** on everything (most watch muted).
- ✅ Same intro line + same CTA style every time.
- ✅ Record in short **1-2 min segments**.

## Screen-share rules (🖥️)
- Browser/IDE zoom **110-125%** so it's readable on a phone.
- **Hide notifications** (Slack, email, OS toasts). Enable Do Not Disturb.
- **Highlight the cursor** / use DemoCreator's click-spotlight.
- Show **one thing at a time**; don't scroll fast.
- Pre-open all tabs/windows before recording so there's no fumbling.

---

## Reel repurposing rule
Every YouTube video → slice **2-3 reels**. Never create a reel from scratch if a video covers it.
